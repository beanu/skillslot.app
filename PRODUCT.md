# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers who use multiple AI coding agents and have accumulated local Agent Skills across tools, directories, and projects. They want to find, understand, organize, and deploy those Skills without manually copying folders or remembering tool-specific paths.

## Product Purpose

SkillSlot is a local-first macOS app that collects Agent Skills into one Vault, groups them into reusable Loadouts, and deploys them to supported agents and custom workspaces. The website should help visitors understand that workflow quickly, try the product without an account, and choose a one-time license when they need the paid limits removed.

## Brand Personality

Focused, refined, reliable. The voice is concise and concrete, with the calm confidence of a well-made native Mac utility.

## Anti-references

Avoid presenting SkillSlot as a giant platform, an all-in-one AI suite, or a cloud account product. Avoid vague SaaS claims, unrelated developer-tool capabilities, and copy that hides what remains available in the free mode.

## Design Principles

- Show the real local Skill workflow instead of describing abstract productivity gains.
- Keep each claim traceable to a shipping product capability.
- Make local-first behavior, no-account setup, and one-time pricing easy to verify.
- Use consistent product terms: Skill, Vault, Loadout, Agent, and License.
- Keep the experience compact and polished, like the desktop app it represents.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.1 AA as the baseline. Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, bilingual Chinese and English copy, and reduced-motion alternatives for non-essential animation.
